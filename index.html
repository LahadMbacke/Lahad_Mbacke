Research Focus

My doctoral work develops frugal, efficient NLP pipelines for automatically extracting and structuring cultural event data from unstructured text — bridging cultural industries with audiences without the computational overhead of large-scale models.

Core areas include named entity recognition, entity linking, information extraction, knowledge graph construction, and low-resource NLP.
